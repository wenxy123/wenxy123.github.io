About me

I am a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Epidemiology & Biostatistics at the University of California, San Francisco, working with Dr. Fei Jiang.

I received my PhD in Statistics from Renmin University of China, advised by Prof. Liping Zhu, and was a visiting PhD student at the London School of Economics and Political Science with Prof. Zoltán Szabó.
